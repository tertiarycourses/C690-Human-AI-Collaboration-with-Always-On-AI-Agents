## About the course

You play the founder-CEO of **Kopi Kaki Co.**, a fictional Singapore kopi startup opening its Tiong Bahru flagship on 1 December 2026, and you use AI agents to help start it. People keep the goals, the judgement and every approval.

Day 1 begins with a brief history of AI, what agentic AI is and how an agent works under the hood. You then compare the always-on "Big Five" assistants by the model inside and what to use each for, and set up **Meta Muse** or **OpenAI Dots** as your CEO assistant. In the afternoon you hire your first AI employee, a **Hermes Agent**, entirely in the Hermes Desktop app: install it, hand it the company rulebook (AGENTS.md), give it a model (your **ChatGPT Plus** plan or Nous Portal's free **Solar Mini 4**) and a Telegram bot, then use tools and skills for Gmail, web design, PPTX and Firecrawl web search. Working alone, it builds the Kopi Kaki website and a professional pitch deck.

Day 2 moves from one agent to a team. You hire five named bots and put them in one **group chat** to write the LinkedIn, Facebook and Instagram launch posts. One prompt then builds a **video production team on a Kanban board** (research, script, voice-over, render, review, then your approval), and **scheduled jobs (crons)** set both teams to work every week, still behind your approval. The day closes with grounding/RAG, evaluation metrics and the five principles of AI security. An optional bonus topic runs Kopi Kaki as a **one-person company in Paperclip**.

## Learning outcomes

- **LO1:** Analyse AI (LLM-based) applications across a range of industries to identify their capabilities and limitations.
- **LO2:** Establish the relationship between AI algorithm/agent design and chatbot/agent efficiency.
- **LO3:** Evaluate and improve the effectiveness of AI (RAG and agent) applications in product development.

## Topics covered

1. **Overview of Always-On AI Agents and Human-AI Collaboration.** AI history and agentic AI; under the hood (the brain-and-body analogy, the agentic loop); five collaboration scenarios; use cases; the Big Five always-on agents (Meta Muse, Claude Duet, OpenAI Dots, Manus Cue, Grok Bots) compared by model and use case; Activity 1 with Meta Muse or OpenAI Dots.
2. **Designing and Building a Single Autonomous AI Agent with Hermes.** Your first AI employee: what an AI agent is; what Hermes is; install Hermes Desktop and attach the company folder; provider and model; Telegram by QR code or BotFather; voice; tools vs skills, with Gmail, web design and PPTX; Firecrawl; scheduled jobs; Activities 2 and 3 (single agent).
3. **Designing Multi-Agent Teams to Improve Workflow Efficiency.** Hermes teams: hub-and-spoke, task decomposition and subagents; bots and group chats (Activity 4); video tools and the Kanban board (Activity 5); content crons (Activity 6); grounding and RAG; evaluation; five principles of AI security.
4. **Bonus (optional) — Paperclip: Kopi Kaki as a One-Person Company.** Install Paperclip; set up the company and the CEO; the CEO hires a Chief of Staff, who hires the marketing team; the org chart; the team makes the video; compare with Hermes Kanban.
